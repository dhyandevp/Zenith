## Design Constraints

The interface must NOT resemble:

• Generic SaaS templates
• Bootstrap
• Material Design
• Tailwind UI examples
• Startup landing pages
• AI-generated dashboard templates

The interface SHOULD resemble a premium consumer product.

Think:

Apple hardware presentation.

Arc Browser.

Readwise.

Teenage Engineering.

Aesop.

Nothing.

Braun.

The visual identity must be unique enough that a screenshot is immediately recognizable as Zenith.

Do not imitate these products directly. Use them only as references for quality, restraint, and craftsmanship.
