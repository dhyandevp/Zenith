# Zenith
## Market Requirements Document (MRD)

Version: 2.0
Status: Approved
Owner: Product Team

---

# Executive Summary

Zenith is a premium digital memory platform that intelligently transforms everything users discover on the internet into a beautiful, searchable, and organized personal library.

Unlike traditional bookmark managers, Zenith is designed around visual organization, automatic metadata enrichment, and effortless rediscovery.

Zenith creates a new product category:

**Digital Memory Platform**

---

# Vision

To become the world's most beautiful place to save, organize, and rediscover everything people find online.

Zenith should become as natural for saving internet content as Apple Photos is for memories.

---

# Mission

Help users never lose valuable information again by replacing fragmented bookmarking habits with one intelligent, visually curated digital home.

---

# Problem Statement

Modern users save information everywhere:

- Browser Bookmarks
- Screenshots
- Telegram Saved Messages
- WhatsApp
- Discord
- Notes
- Pinterest
- Instagram Saved
- Reddit Saved
- YouTube Watch Later

This creates digital clutter.

People remember saving something but cannot remember where.

Finding saved content becomes frustrating.

---

# Market Opportunity

Internet users consume thousands of pieces of content every month.

Current solutions focus on storage.

Zenith focuses on memory.

There is no dominant product combining:

- Automatic understanding
- Beautiful presentation
- Universal content support
- Intelligent organization
